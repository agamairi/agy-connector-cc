Pass 4 also expanded the regression suite around the runtime boundary: every observed file/search/edit tool, unknown and partial events, read-only brain writes, usage variants, progress logs, elapsed-time boundaries, process escalation, concurrent jobs, workspace isolation, and corrupt state/job files are exercised with behavior-sensitive assertions.

Pass 5 adds behavior-sensitive coverage for dead-PID and PID-less reaping, PID-reuse rejection, inactivity termination, progress timestamps, lowered timeout defaults, persistent async results, and read-only versus write-capable agy posture.

Pass 6 restores the old connector's per-run quality controls without adding invocations: write-capable tasks must verify their work and report the checks, while read-only work remains lean. Write tasks default to `gemini-3.1-pro-low`; research and review default to `gemini-3.7-flash-low`. `AGY_DEFAULT_MODEL` and explicit `--model` selection are supported without adding a default-path `agy models` network call, and the actual model is persisted and rendered.

The remaining divergences are intentional and architectural: Antigravity spawns `agy` and parses NDJSON because it has no app server, cancellation therefore targets the worker process group rather than an app-server turn, review is schema-guided rather than native, and transfer is inert context replay rather than native session import. No app server, broker, interrupt API, native reviewer, or native importer is emulated.
